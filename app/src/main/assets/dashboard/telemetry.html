Off by default. Records the complete telemetry snapshot the scooter sends, once per minute while you ride: speed, gear, battery, voltage, current, power, temperatures, trip and total distance and any active fault codes. Recording starts as soon as the scooter first moves. All rides are kept until you delete them.
